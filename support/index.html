Graphe Support

How can we help?

Report a problem, flag a Bible content issue, or share an idea for Graphe Bible Games.

Send us a note

Use our feedback form to reach Graphe Support. Include an email address only if you would like a response.

Open feedback form

Opens Google Forms. Submitting feedback is optional.

What to include

For a problem, tell us which game or screen you were using, what happened, and what you expected. Your device model, iOS version, and Graphe version (shown in Settings) can help us investigate.

For a Bible content issue, include the question or clue and any relevant Scripture reference. Please avoid passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal details.

Have a privacy request?

Use the same form to ask a privacy question or request access, correction, or deletion of information you previously submitted.

  1. Select Other as the feedback type.
  2. Select General as the part of Graphe.
  3. Start your message with Privacy request and describe what you need.
  4. Include a reply email if you want a response. For a previous submission, its approximate date and the email you used can help us find it.

Send a privacy request via Google Forms →

See the Privacy Policy for more about local game storage, feedback, retention, and deletion.

About your saved progress

Graphe saves progress and preferences locally and does not offer accounts or its own cloud sync. Deleting the app can remove your progress. Device backups may retain or restore data separately. If the app reports a save error, try the in-app retry option before closing it.
