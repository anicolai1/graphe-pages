Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Graphe Bible Games (“Graphe,” “we,” or “us”) is the public-facing operator of the Graphe Bible Games app. This policy explains how information is handled in the app, through optional feedback, and on this website. Our contact identity is Graphe Support.

Your game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Graphe does not require an account, provide its own cloud sync, or include advertising or gameplay analytics services. Sending feedback is optional and uses an external Google Form.

Game data on your device

Graphe saves Daily Challenge progress, answers, results, scores, completion dates, streaks, Free Play history, and preferences such as your default difficulty in local app storage. These records let you continue a challenge and review your statistics. Graphe does not upload these gameplay records to a server operated by us.

Graphe does not offer account registration or app-managed cloud synchronization. Your device’s backup or transfer services may separately include app data, depending on your settings. For example, Apple explains how iCloud Backup handles app data.

Notifications

Optional local reminders for the Daily Challenge are planned. The current version does not implement reminders or register for remote push notifications. We will update this policy when reminders become available to describe their permissions, settings, and data handling.

Optional feedback and support

The app and this website link to a feedback form hosted by Google Forms. Opening the link takes you to Google’s service; submitting feedback is not required to play.

If you submit the form, Graphe Support receives the feedback category, the part of the app you select, your message, and any email address you choose to enter for a response. We use this information to review feedback, investigate issues, improve Graphe, and respond to support or privacy requests.

Please share only what is needed. Do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information. An email address is optional, but we need a reply address if you want a response.

Google processes form submissions and may process technical information about your visit under its own Privacy Policy. Google’s processing and retention are separate from the local game data stored by Graphe.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains in app storage until it is removed by you or the operating system. On iOS, deleting the app generally removes its local data; offloading an app keeps its documents and data. Device backups may retain copies or restore them later. Manage those copies through your device or backup provider.

Feedback is stored separately from the app, so deleting the app does not delete a form submission. We keep feedback and correspondence only as long as needed to address the request and maintain relevant support records, or as required by law. You may request deletion through Graphe Support using the steps below. We cannot remotely erase game records held only on your device or control retention by Google, Apple, or GitHub.

Privacy questions and requests

Contact Graphe Support through our existing feedback form:

  1. Choose Other for “What type of feedback is this?”
  2. Choose General for “Which part of Graphe?”
  3. Start “Tell us more” with Privacy request, then describe your question or request, such as access, correction, or deletion of a previous submission.
  4. If you want a response, enter a reply address in “Email Address (If a response is requested).”

To help us find a previous submission, include its approximate date and any email address you supplied with it. We may ask for limited additional information to verify a request before sharing or deleting information.

Children’s privacy

Gameplay does not ask for a name, email address, or account. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support and should not submit personal information through the feedback form. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information can request its deletion using the privacy-request steps above.

This website and platform services

This website uses no added analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, or embedded feedback forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation and Privacy Statement.

Apple may separately process App Store, TestFlight, device backup, or diagnostic information under its services and your settings. See Apple’s privacy information. These platform services are separate from Graphe’s local gameplay storage.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when Graphe’s features or information handling change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Privacy questions can always be sent to Graphe Support through the form linked above.
